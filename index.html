Calendar-first features

Calendar View

See past and future work by month with color-coded events, list filters, and a fast jump between calendars.

Scan Calendar

Capture a calendar, crop it, and let Star Task turn visible events into reminders instead of retyping a packed schedule by hand.

Unstoppable Reminders

Cross-platform reminders repeat on your schedule, with one-tap silence for movies or meetings and restore when you are ready again.

Upcoming Controls

Review deadlines, filter by list, and use device-level controls to silence, restore, or permanently delete reminder schedules.

AI to Calendar

Use Text, Voice, Documents, or Links to generate tasks, summaries, brainstorms, and reminder lists that land in your daily plan.

Live Sharing

Share lists for family plans, school schedules, events, projects, and chores while keeping task order synced in the cloud.

Calendar Personalization

Use list colors, themes, text sizing, reminder sounds, snooze length, mic FAB options, and custom backgrounds to make planning yours.

Profile & Stats

Track total lists, tasks, AI-powered reminders, logins, subscription status, and owned Star Chaos themes.

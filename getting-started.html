Welcome to Star Task Calendar ✨

Scan schedules, track due dates, and let AI turn life into reminders.

How it works 🧭

  • 🗓️ Open Upcoming to see your calendar view, past + future filter, and list colors.
  • 📸 Tap Scan Calendar when a paper schedule or screenshot needs to become reminders.
  • ➕ Tap + to create a manual list when you want to build a calendar from scratch.
  • 🤖 Use the AI button anytime to generate lists, summaries, brainstorms, or reminder plans.

AI quick start ⚡

  • ⌨️ AI Text — type what you need (e.g., “Turn this practice schedule into reminders”).
  • 🎙️ AI Voice — speak naturally; we’ll turn it into calendar-ready tasks. Perfect for meetings or memory dumps.
  • 🔗 AI Link — paste a URL; we’ll fetch & summarize into tasks.
  • 📄 AI Document — pick a file; we’ll extract & build a list.

Reminders ⏰

  • 🤖 AI can auto-set Auto / Banner + Sound / Banner (Silent) based on your prompt.
  • 📣 Banner + Sound — pick from two alert sounds.
  • 🔕 Banner (Silent) — gentle nudge, no blast.
  • 🛠️ Manage everything in Settings → Reminders (Silence / Restore / Delete).
  • 🎬 Tap Silence before a movie, class, or work meeting, then Restore afterward to bring your schedule back.
  • 🗓️ Check Upcoming to see which reminders are active on the calendar.

Tip: you can switch the AI reminder mode anytime inside the AI tab (Auto / Banner + Sound / Banner Silent).

Calendar pro tips 💡

  • 🎨 Use calendar list colors so packed days stay readable at a glance.
  • 🔍 Filter to one calendar/list when you need a cleaner schedule view.
  • 🔗 Subtasks support clickable links — great for references.
  • 🧬 Duplicate tasks (clearly labeled) to reuse patterns.
  • 🧲 Merge Lists from the dashboard (Edit mode).
  • ↕️ Reorder lists and tasks with drag handles.

Themes & Star Chaos 🌌

  • 🎨 Tap the Themes icon in the bottom bar. Most are Premium; Cosmic Dawn is free.
  • 📊 Check Profile/Stats to see subscription status and purchases.
  • 🖼️ Upload a custom background with Premium or the Custom Background purchase.

Sharing 🤝

  • 📤 Invite others to a Shared List and manage members/permissions.

Good to know 🧩

  • 📴 Reminders persist & restore after reboots.
  • 📸 Calendar scans can preserve the original captured image where supported.
  • 🌍 AI supports English on iOS right now (more languages planned).
  • 🔒 Privacy-respecting by default — you control what gets saved.

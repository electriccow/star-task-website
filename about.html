Star Task · Calendar-first vision

Built for schedules that refuse to stay simple.

Star Task is a handcrafted calendar and reminder app powered by AI. It turns calendar photos, voice, text, links, and documents into clean, actionable reminders, then keeps everything visible in a color-coded calendar and Upcoming view.

Our Vision

Calendar planning should feel beautiful and alive, not heavy. Star Task focuses on signal over noise: fast schedule capture, respectful reminders, and a UI that keeps the next thing obvious. It’s your space to plan, think, and execute.

We care about thoughtful details: Scan Calendar, color-coded list legends, drag-to-reorder lists, rapid-fire task entry, label management, sharing that just works, and themes that make everyday planning feel like a ritual instead of a chore.

How We Think

Star Task is an indie, heart-forward product. We build for real people with real calendars, meetings, family, goals, and chaos. AI should reduce friction, never add it. Every feature asks: does this make life easier right now?

Privacy matters. You can delete content, delete your account, and even bring your own OpenAI API key for advanced models.

Calendar AI that Listens

Five practical ways in: Scan Calendar, Text, Voice (Quick Command or Record & Transcribe), Document, and Link. Pick your List Type (Bullet, Reminder, Summary, Brainstorm) and let Star Task do the heavy lifting.

Reminders can be Banner + Sound or Banner (Silent), repeating on your schedule and updating next-due times from the cloud.

Themes & Feel

Start with Cosmic Dawn, then level up with Daybreak, Alive, Elemental Green, Charge, Peach, Rejuvenating Whisper, and Withering Whisper. Light/Dark/System modes, plus custom background uploads.

Star Chaos animations bring a little starlight every time you open the app.

Scan Calendar

When a schedule already exists on paper, in a screenshot, or in a calendar photo, Star Task helps you turn that visual chaos into usable reminders. Capture, crop, scan, and let AI convert visible events into tasks you can review and keep.

  • Use the camera or an existing calendar image as the source.
  • Crop the calendar before scanning so the AI focuses on the right schedule.
  • See progress while Star Task reads events, creates reminders, and saves them into your plan.
  • Keep a backup of captured calendar photos where supported, so one-time calendars are not lost after scanning.

AI Modal · Four Inputs, Calendar-Ready Output

Tap the AI button to open a sleek bottom sheet with four input tabs and list types that can become tasks, summaries, brainstorms, or reminders.

Inputs

  • AI Text: paste or type, then submit.
  • AI Voice: Quick Command (instant) or Record & Transcribe (full note-taking). Shows remaining minutes and lets you pick audio files.
  • AI Document: upload common document formats and auto-generate a list.
  • AI Link: paste a URL and turn it into tasks.

List Types

  • Bullet Point List: classic task list; toggle optional notes; choose 5 / 10 / 15 items.
  • Reminder List: sub-dropdown for Auto, Banner + Sound, Banner (Silent), ready for Upcoming and Calendar.
  • Summary List: condense content into actionable tasks.
  • Brainstorm List: expand ideas with chip counts (10 / 14 / 20).

Lists & List View

  • Cards that feel great: tap any task to open details.
  • Edit Mode: duplicate, delete, or copy a task’s name + notes + subtasks to clipboard.
  • AI Suggestions: get ~10+ task ideas based on the list name; tap Add to insert.
  • Rapid Add: an outlined text field under the top bar for fast, continuous entry.
  • More Options: sort (name / due / priority / number), copy the whole list as text, collapse long tasks, reorder with cloud-saved order for shared lists, and hide/show completed.

Task Detail

  • Edit name, due date, priority, attachment thumbnail, and labels.
  • Label Manager: create/delete global labels; add/remove task labels.

Unstoppable Cross-Platform Reminders

Star Task’s reminders connect the calendar, task details, and Upcoming screen so a due date is never just hidden in one view. They are built for real interruptions: silence everything before a movie, meeting, class, or quiet shift, then restore reminders when you are ready.

  • Cross-platform control: manage reminder state across iOS and Android workflows instead of babysitting each task one by one.
  • Types: Don’t remind or Remind when due, then choose Banner + Sound or Banner (Silent).
  • Frequency: hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, every 10 minutes, or every 30 minutes.
  • Repeat Duration: default Forever or pick 1–24 hours. Banners stop after the window even if you don’t interact.
  • Auto-update: dismissing updates the task’s due date + reminder in Firestore, so Upcoming and Calendar stay accurate.
  • Note: iOS banners respect Do Not Disturb.

Upcoming & Device Controls

  • See all tasks sorted by earliest due date, with a days-until counter.
  • Filter by list with a simple dropdown.
  • Use color-coded calendar legend dots to understand where each event came from at a glance.
  • Device Controls: Silence all reminders on this device before a movie or meeting, Restore all reminders from cloud afterward, or Permanently delete all reminders from cloud.
  • Tasks show Reminder Active when a local reminder is running on the device.
